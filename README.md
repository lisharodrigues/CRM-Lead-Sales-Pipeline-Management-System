# CRM Lead & Sales Pipeline Management System

## Project Overview

The CRM Lead & Sales Pipeline Management System is a Business Analysis project focused on improving lead management, follow-up tracking, and sales pipeline visibility.

The project addresses common challenges faced by sales teams, such as missed follow-ups, inconsistent lead status updates, and limited visibility into sales performance. It proposes a structured CRM workflow to help Business Development Executives manage leads efficiently and enable Sales Managers to monitor pipeline progress.

The project covers the Business Analysis lifecycle, including requirement gathering, process modelling, documentation, data analysis, dashboard design, and user acceptance testing.

## Business Problem

Sales teams may encounter the following challenges:

- Missed or delayed follow-ups with potential customers.
- Limited visibility into lead status and sales pipeline progress.
- Difficulty monitoring individual sales executive performance.
- Inconsistent lead status updates and follow-up records.
- Limited access to key performance indicators for decision-making.

These challenges can affect lead conversion and make it difficult for managers to identify bottlenecks in the sales process.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed CRM solution aims to centralize lead information, standardize lead qualification, track follow-up activities, and improve sales pipeline monitoring.

Key features include:

- Centralized lead creation and assignment.
- Standardized lead qualification and status tracking.
- Follow-up history and reminders.
- Sales pipeline stage tracking.
- Sales Manager visibility into team performance.
- SQL-based data analysis and a Power BI dashboard for monitoring key business metrics.

## Project Objectives

- Understand stakeholder needs and document business requirements.
- Identify gaps in the existing lead management process.
- Design an improved To-Be business process.
- Define functional and non-functional requirements.
- Develop user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Analyze lead and sales data using SQL and Excel.
- Define business KPIs and design a Power BI dashboard.
- Prepare UAT scenarios to validate business requirements.

## Proposed Business Process

1. **Lead Creation:** The Sales Executive creates a lead with the required contact and business details.
2. **Lead Assignment:** The lead is assigned to a responsible Sales Executive.
3. **Lead Qualification:** The executive evaluates the lead against defined business qualification criteria.
4. **Follow-up Management:** Contact attempts, updates, and next follow-up activities are recorded. Reminders help reduce missed follow-ups.
5. **Pipeline Management:** Qualified leads progress through relevant sales stages, such as requirement understanding, solution discussion, proposal, and negotiation.
6. **Lead Closure:** The lead is marked as Converted or Lost based on the outcome.
7. **Performance Monitoring:** The Sales Manager reviews pipeline progress, follow-up activities, and conversion metrics.

## Key Business KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total Leads | Total number of leads recorded in the system. |
| Qualified Leads | Number of leads meeting the defined qualification criteria. |
| Converted Leads | Number of leads successfully converted. |
| Lead Conversion Rate | (Converted Leads / Total Leads) × 100 |
| Lost Leads | Number of leads closed without conversion. |
| Pending Follow-ups | Number of leads requiring follow-up. |
| Overdue Follow-ups | Number of follow-ups that have passed their scheduled date without completion. |
| Pipeline Distribution | Number of leads across different sales pipeline stages. |

**Business Rule:** Lead Conversion Rate is calculated using total leads as the denominator, rather than only qualified leads.

## Expected Business Benefits

The proposed solution is intended to:

- Reduce missed follow-ups through activity tracking and reminders.
- Improve visibility into lead status and pipeline progress.
- Standardize lead qualification and sales stage updates.
- Help managers identify pipeline bottlenecks.
- Support data-driven decisions through KPI reporting.
- Improve accountability through lead ownership and activity history.

These are expected benefits of the proposed solution, not measured results from a live implementation.
